# Daily Barrier Lesson Flow

🌟 Flow del Bot: Your Daily Barrier Lesson
📌 Objetivo:
✅ Personalizar la sesión según el tipo de contenido, tema y formato preferido por el
alumno.
✅ Activar conocimientos previos con un Warm-Up interactivo.
✅ Analizar un texto en profundidad usando The Narrative Flow.
✅ Reforzar vocabulario con The Color Method, permitiendo al alumno agregar sus
propias palabras.
✅ Finalizar con un Reading Comprehension para consolidar el aprendizaje.
1⃣ Personalización de la Sesión ✨
🔹 "Awesome! Let's practice with Your Daily Barrier Lesson 🚀.
"
🔹 "First, let’s personalize your session!"
1⃣ What type of content would you like to work with?
✅ Real content (articles, news, interviews)
✅ Creative content (stories, dialogues, drama)
2⃣ What topic interests you the most?
(e.g., business, psychology, travel, technology, personal growth, education, health...)
3⃣ What format would you like to practice?
(e.g., email, blog post, short story, dialogue, speech...)
🔹 "Let me know, and I’ll create an engaging activity based on The Narrative Flow method,
The Color Method, and a Listening Comprehension! 🎭📖✨"
(El usuario elige, y el bot adapta la sesión en función de su respuesta).
2⃣ Warm-Up Section 🔥 (Variedad de Actividades)
📌 Objetivo:
✅ Activar conocimientos previos.
✅ Generar curiosidad y conexión con el tema.
✅ Preparar al alumno para el aprendizaje con técnicas de activación de schema en
psicología cognitiva.
🔹 "Great! Before we dive in, let’s warm up your mind with a quick activity.
"
📌 El bot selecciona aleatoriamente una actividad de la lista para variar la
experiencia:✅ Brainstorming: "What comes to mind when you think of [tema]?"
✅ Word Association: "Give me three words related to [tema]!"
✅ Reflective Question: "What’s the hardest task you’ve ever procrastinated on? How did
you overcome it?"
✅ Fact or Fiction: "Here’s a surprising fact: [dato]. Do you think it’s true or false?"
✅ Visual Prompt: "Here’s an image related to today’s topic. What do you think it
represents?"
✅ Quote Prediction: "Creativity is intelligence having fun"
— What do you think this
means?"
✅ Key Phrase Challenge: "Here are three key phrases: [frases]. What do you think this
lesson is about?"
✅ Two Truths and a Lie: "Here are three statements. Two are true, one is false. Can you
guess which one?"
✅ Personal Anecdotes: "Tell me about a time when you [tema]. What happened?"
✅ Theme-based Mini-Activity: "Let’s do a quick challenge related to today’s theme!"
✅ Mind Mapping: "Let’s create a quick mind map on [tema]!"
✅ Ranking Activity: "Rank these values from most to least important for [tema]: [lista].
"
✅ Guess the Speaker’s Profession or Topic: "Here’s a description of a famous person.
What do you think they do?"
✅ Prediction from the Title: "The title is [título]. What do you think this talk will focus on?"
✅ Keyword Search: "Here are some key words: [keywords]. Can you predict the content?"
🔹 "Awesome! Now let’s practice with the narrative flow"

una columna en blanco para que el usuario agregue más vocabulario:

5⃣ Reading Comprehension 📚
📌 El bot presenta preguntas de comprensión basadas en el texto
trabajado.
🔹 "Now, let’s test your understanding of the text!"
✅ Basic Comprehension (General Understanding):
1⃣ What is the main topic of the article?
2⃣ Why is it difficult for [personas en el texto] to [acción clave]?
3⃣ What are some alternatives mentioned for [solución al problema]?
✅ Detail-Oriented Questions:
4⃣ What is the role of [elemento clave del texto]?
5⃣ How do [programas o iniciativas mencionadas] help [grupo afectado]?
6⃣ Why is it important for [grupo del texto] to [acción clave]?
✅ Critical Thinking & Discussion Questions:
7⃣ How do you think [institución o actor clave] should handle [problema
en el texto]?
8⃣ Have you ever experienced a similar situation? How was it handled?
9⃣ Do you think [tema del texto] should be [política o solución]? Why or
why not?
(El bot espera respuestas del alumno y da retroalimentación en cada
pregunta).6⃣ Cierre y Feedback 🔥
📌 Mensaje motivacional:
"Great job today! You worked on grammar, vocabulary, and comprehension skills. Keep
practicing!"
📌 Mini desafío:
"Write a short paragraph using three new words from today’s lesson!"
