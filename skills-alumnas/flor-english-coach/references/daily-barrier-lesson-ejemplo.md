🎯 Objectives
By the end of this lesson, students will:
✅ Identify and use Present Simple, Past Simple, Present Continuous, and Present
Perfect.
✅ Recognize and apply phrasal verbs and idioms in context.
✅ Use The Color Method to categorize vocabulary.
✅ Apply The Narrative Flow Method in three steps to analyze verb tenses.
✅ Write their own short essay using the learned structures.

🐶 The Health Benefits of Having Pets
Having a pet brings joy, companionship, and even unexpected health benefits.
People who own animals develop a stronger sense of responsibility and raise
awareness about animal welfare. Studies show that spending time with pets reduces
stress, lifts spirits, and keeps loneliness at bay. Right now, many scientists are
working on research that explores the connection between pets and mental health.
It’s no surprise that having a furry friend is making people feel happier every day.

therapy programs took off years ago, and since then, hospitals have brought in

loving homes for abandoned animals, and adoption rates have gone through the
roof. Last year, a new trend caught on—people started fostering pets during
lockdowns, realizing how much joy animals can bring.

✍ Step 5: Guided Writing Activity
📌 Task: Write a short paragraph (6-8 sentences) about the benefits of pets for
mental health.
🔹 Follow these steps:
1⃣ Describe a pet and how it improves a person’s life (Use Present Simple).
2⃣ Mention a past event related to a pet (Use Past Simple).
3⃣ Explain something that is happening right now about pets (Use Present
Continuous).
4⃣ Talk about an experience or trend that has been happening for some time (Use
Present Perfect).
5⃣ Use at least two phrasal verbs and one idiom from today’s lesson.
🔹 Example start:
"Many people believe that pets bring happiness and reduce stress. I remember that
last year my friend adopted a dog, and it changed his life. Right now, more people
are adopting pets than ever before. Over the past few years, studies have shown
that pets improve mental health. Taking care of an animal is not a walk in the park,
but it is worth it!"
🔹 Peer Review: Exchange essays with a partner and highlight each verb tense.
🎤 Step 6: Speaking & Reflection - Shadowing (10 min)
📌 Shadowing Activity:

2⃣ Repeat each sentence immediately after, mimicking pronunciation, intonation, and
rhythm.
3⃣ Focus on fluency and try to sound as natural as possible.
