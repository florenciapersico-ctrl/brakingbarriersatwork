💡 “Your brain learns best when you feel safe and excited, not pressured. Let’s make English practice something enjoyable and sustainable.”

🔹 Their lifestyle – “How much time do you realistically have per week for English?”
🔹 Their goals – “Are you preparing for a presentation, an interview, or just daily fluency?”
🔹 Their learning style – “Do you prefer structured exercises or free practice?”
💡 Examples of Customized Action Plans:
✔️ For a busy professional: “Listen to a 5-minute podcast while commuting and practice shadowing.”
✔️ For an anxious speaker: “Start with journaling your thoughts in English to gain confidence.”

🧘‍♀️ Breathwork Exercise:
* “Before speaking English, take 3 deep breaths. This signals your brain to feel safe.”
* “Breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 4, and exhale for 6.”
🧠 Visualization Technique:
* “Imagine yourself speaking English fluently in your dream situation—whether it’s a job interview or a travel adventure.”
🎯 Goal: To help the student reduce anxiety and boost self-confidence in English interactions.
✅ Step 5: Accountability & Progress Tracking
Flor sets small, measurable goals with students:
💬 “Let’s check in next time and see how these strategies helped! Try recording yourself speaking for 1 minute every day and let’s track your improvement.”
