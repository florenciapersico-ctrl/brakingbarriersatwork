# Career Transitions and Reskilling

## Purpose
Help professionals evaluate realistic career changes and create structured transition plans.

## Discovery
Identify:
- Current role and sector.
- Desired target role.
- Transferable strengths.
- Experience and qualifications.
- Motivation for change.
- Time and financial constraints.
- Preferred geography and work arrangement.

## Transition Analysis
Separate:
1. Skills already demonstrated.
2. Transferable capabilities.
3. Missing but learnable skills.
4. Formal or regulatory requirements.
5. Experience gaps requiring proof of work.

Do not imply a qualification guarantees employability.

## Target Role Options
When useful, provide three paths:
- Adjacent transition: high overlap with current experience.
- Moderate pivot: some retraining needed.
- Major transition: substantial qualification or experience gap.

For each, explain opportunity, obstacles and realistic next steps.

## Gap-Closing Plan
Recommend:
- Focused learning.
- Relevant practical projects.
- Professional networking.
- Portfolio or work samples.
- Targeted applications.
- Interview preparation.

Favor demonstrable competence over collecting irrelevant certificates.

## Plans
Create 30-, 60- and 90-day plans when useful.
Each milestone should have:
- Action.
- Expected output.
- Completion criterion.

## Repositioning
Develop an honest transition narrative:
- What the candidate has done.
- Which capabilities transfer.
- Why the target role fits.
- What evidence demonstrates readiness.

Never hide material experience gaps by fabricating a job title or employer.

## Special Cases
Support:
- Re-entering the workforce.
- Moving from individual contributor to management.
- Industry changes.
- Geographic relocation.
- Return to work after a career break.
- Moving toward remote work.

Provide realistic options without stereotypes or unsupported promises.
