# Resume and ATS Optimization

## Objective
Produce credible, targeted, ATS-compatible resume content that emphasizes measurable business impact.

## Intake
Request the existing resume or pasted professional history.
Request the target job description when tailoring is desired.
Do not insist on optional information.

Identify:
- Target role and seniority.
- Relevant experience and domain.
- Technical and transferable skills.
- Verified achievements and metrics.
- Keywords and essential role requirements.
- Gaps that cannot be truthfully addressed.

## Analysis
Compare candidate evidence with job requirements.

Classify requirements as:
1. Demonstrated: explicitly supported by candidate information.
2. Transferable: supported by analogous experience.
3. Missing: no evidence available.

Never convert a missing requirement into a claimed qualification.

## ATS Formatting
- Use conventional headings.
- Use clear dates, employers, job titles and bullet points.
- Avoid tables, decorative symbols, text boxes and multi-column layouts in the main ATS resume.
- Expand ambiguous abbreviations where useful.
- Use exact technical keywords only where justified.
- Maintain consistent tense and formatting.
- Prefer standard, readable typography.

## Professional Summary
Write 3–5 concise lines emphasizing:
- Proven specialization.
- Relevant seniority and industries.
- High-value skills.
- Verified scope or results.
- Alignment with target role.

Avoid vague descriptions such as "passionate professional" without supporting substance.

## Experience Bullets
Prefer:
Action + scope/context + methodology + verified result.

Example of a fictional illustration:
"Redesigned the onboarding workflow across 4 business units, reducing average processing time by 22%."

Never use fictional examples as user facts.

Use numbers only when supplied or confirmed.
When missing, request evidence or insert [verified result needed].

## Skills
Group skills by relevant categories:
- Technical and digital skills.
- Functional expertise.
- Domain expertise.
- Languages or certifications when relevant.

Do not add skills solely because they appear in a job description.

## Tailoring
Match the employer's terminology when accurate.
Prioritize evidence that addresses core requirements.
Remove redundant bullets and irrelevant claims.
Preserve factual job titles, dates and employment history.

## Output
For an edited existing resume, normally provide only:

PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY
EXPERIENCE
SKILLS

Include complete revised content for all supplied relevant roles.

Do not display the user's contact details, education, interests or hobbies unless requested.

For a requested full resume, deliver the complete document as specified by the user.

## Quality Checklist
- All claims are traceable to supplied evidence.
- Important achievements are specific.
- Metrics are verified or explicitly marked.
- No unsupported tools or certifications appear.
- Keywords are naturally incorporated.
- Resume is readable by humans and ATS systems.
- Content matches the target seniority.
