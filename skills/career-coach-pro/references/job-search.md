# Job Search and Job Matching

## Purpose
Help users discover suitable, current and verifiable employment opportunities.

## Intake
Establish:
- Target role or acceptable job families.
- Location, country or remote preference.
- Desired seniority.
- Relevant experience and skills.

Optional:
- Resume.
- Language proficiency.
- Salary expectations and currency.
- Employment type.
- Time zone restrictions.
- Work authorization.

Do not repeatedly request already known information.

## Research
When live browsing is available:
1. Search official company career pages.
2. Search relevant job boards.
3. Open promising individual postings.
4. Validate that the URL leads to the specific opportunity.
5. Check the location and remote eligibility.
6. Extract requirements and publication details.
7. Deduplicate reposted opportunities.
8. Compare confirmed requirements with the candidate profile.

Prioritize:
- Direct employer application pages.
- Currently open positions.
- Strong skill alignment.
- Eligible locations.
- Relevant seniority.
- Verified language requirements.

## Job Board Sources
Examples to investigate, not guaranteed current sources:
- Company career websites.
- LinkedIn Jobs.
- Indeed.
- Wellfound.
- We Work Remotely.
- Remote OK.
- Remote.co.
- Built In.
- Regional job boards.

Use sources available in the candidate's market.

## Job Result Format
Target 10 verified matches.

For each:
- Job title.
- Company.
- Company description.
- Location.
- Remote / hybrid / onsite arrangement.
- Language requirements.
- Posting date, if verified.
- Required years of experience, if specified.
- Salary range and currency, if disclosed.
- Main responsibilities.
- Direct application URL.
- Fit rationale.

Use "Not specified" for undisclosed fields and "Not verified" when evidence cannot be confirmed.

Do not substitute the company homepage for a direct application link without clearly labeling it.

## Remote Eligibility
Distinguish:
- Remote worldwide.
- Remote within a particular country.
- Remote within a specified region.
- Remote within a time zone.
- Hybrid arrangements.

Never imply that "remote" automatically means the user can work from any country.

## Freshness
Prefer listings with verifiable posting information.
Distinguish publication dates from update or indexing dates.
Do not invent publication dates or report listings as open without evidence.

If fewer than 10 suitable verified jobs are found, show the verified subset and explain the limitation.

## Candidate Fit
Use a qualitative assessment:
- Strong match.
- Partial match.
- Stretch opportunity.

Base the category on essential requirements, not an invented percentage.

Explain relevant strengths and genuine gaps.

## No Browsing
If browsing is not available:
- State that live openings cannot be verified.
- Provide search strings and relevant career portals.
- Offer to assess pasted job postings.
- Never fabricate job listings.

## Remote-Friendly Employer Research
An employer historically associated with remote work is not necessarily currently hiring or recruiting internationally.
Verify current openings and eligibility independently.

## Output
Use an easily scannable comparison table or grouped list.
Every verified opening should have a direct clickable URL.
Prefer useful evidence over filling a target count.
