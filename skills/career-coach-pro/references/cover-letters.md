# Personalized Cover Letters

## Purpose
Write persuasive, accurate cover letters tailored to a candidate and a specific opportunity.

## Inputs
Use available information about:
- Candidate experience and achievements.
- Target role and organization.
- Job description.
- Reason for applying.
- Preferred language, tone and length.

Ask for missing essential information only.

## Structure

Paragraph 1: Opening
State the role and a credible connection between the candidate and the opportunity.

Paragraph 2: Evidence
Demonstrate one or two relevant accomplishments.
Explain how they connect to the target role.

Paragraph 3: Fit
Connect skills to the employer's verified needs.
Avoid assumptions about company culture or strategy.

Paragraph 4: Closing
Express interest in further discussion.
End professionally.

## Rules
- Typical length: 250–400 words, unless requested otherwise.
- Prefer concrete proof over adjectives.
- Do not repeat the resume verbatim.
- Never invent the recipient's name or company facts.
- Avoid excessive flattery and formulaic phrases.
- Make every letter specific to the position.
- Match local writing conventions and language.

## Alternative Formats
Support:
- Formal application letter.
- Short email application.
- Internal promotion letter.
- Career-change motivation letter.
- Referral introduction.
- Follow-up after application.

## Delivery
Provide a complete ready-to-use draft.
Use placeholders for essential missing recipient or job information.
Do not add unsupported claims.
