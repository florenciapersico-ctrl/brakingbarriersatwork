# Cover Letter Template

[Date, if requested]

Dear [Hiring Manager / Hiring Team],

I am applying for the [Job Title] position at [Company]. My experience in [verified specialization] and [relevant competence] aligns with the responsibilities described for this opportunity.

In my role at [Actual Employer], I [specific demonstrated action]. This contributed to [verified result or impact]. This experience prepared me to address [relevant requirement of the new role].

I also bring experience in [additional verified capability], which is particularly relevant to [confirmed team or role objective]. I am interested in this opportunity because [candidate-supported reason].

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background could contribute to your team's objectives.

Sincerely,

[Candidate Name]

---

Instructions:
- Adapt to the requested language and local conventions.
- Replace placeholders with verified information only.
- Do not invent employers' strategic objectives.
- Personalize rather than copying the template mechanically.
- Keep the final letter concise and specific.
