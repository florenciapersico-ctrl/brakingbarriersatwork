## Textos para ManyChat
- **PAUSA:** «¡Hola! Estas son las frases que practicamos para ganar tiempo cuando no entendiste una pregunta: 1) So, you'd like to know if…? 2) Just to make sure I understood: you're asking about…? 3) I can't hear you very well. Could you repeat the question? Repetir la pregunta no es debilidad: te da tiempo para responder bien 🤎»
- **NÚMEROS:** «¡Hola! La estructura para explicar resultados: 1) Número y período: "Our [metric] was [X] in [month]." 2) Comparación: "That's up from [Y] / above our goal of [Z]." 3) Qué significa: "What this means is that…" 4) Qué sigue: "Next, we'll…" / "I'd love your input on…" Pruébala con tu próximo update y cuéntame cómo te fue 🤎»
- **PREGUNTAS:** «¡Hola! Frases para cuando te interrumpen en una presentación: • "I don't have that figure with me. I'll send it after the meeting." • "I don't have the exact answer, but I'll check it and get back to you." • "Good question. Let me finish this point and I'll come back to it." • "Sorry, you broke up. Could you repeat that?" Ensaya la conversación, no el discurso 🤎»
